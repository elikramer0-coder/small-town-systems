What we do

Websites

Built for your business, then kept running so you don’t have to think about it.

  • Designed and built for your business. Not a template.
  • Made for phones first. Fast, and easy for everyone to use.
  • A logo, if you need one.
  • Hosting, security, domain and DNS: we handle them.
  • Leaving Hibu or another provider? We move your domain and point your old links to the new pages, so Google keeps finding you.
  • A contact or estimate form that emails you, or prints at your shop if you don’t use email.
  • The Google basics: page titles and descriptions, the business details Google reads, a sitemap and Search Console. Your Google Business Profile fixed and kept current.
  • Printable review cards with a QR code that opens your Google review page.
  • Don’t do tech? You don’t have to. We handle all of it.

Office systems

Notion and Claude, set up so your jobs, customers and paperwork live in one place.

  • Notion, set up as your business’s home base: jobs, to-dos, customers, how-tos and decisions.
  • Claude, an AI assistant, connected so it knows your business. It writes drafts, answers questions, does research and helps with paperwork.
  • We set it up and show you how to use it. We run our own businesses on this exact setup.
