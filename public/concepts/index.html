Three directions for the home page

Each one is a full home page with real copy, working on phones and desktops. Pick the one to build out. Prices and lists with a dotted underline are drafts until confirmed. These pages are hidden from search engines and not linked from the site.

  1. A. Building blocks

    The cube logo becomes the price list. One block is one page, so you can see the size of a website before you read a number.

    Open concept A

  2. B. Itemized

    Every price printed on a shop receipt. Pick a size and see exactly what you pay, line by line, on a steel-siding backdrop.

    Open concept B

  3. C. Big sky

    Quiet and wide like a Kansas horizon. The town picture is cleaned up into a skyline standing on one long line, with serif headings.
