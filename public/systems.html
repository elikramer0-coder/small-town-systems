What it is

Two tools, set up for you

Notion is a board on your phone and your computer. Every job on it: who, where, what, what stage it's in, what's owed.

Claude is an AI helper that sits next to it. It writes the estimate email, the follow-up text, the reminder. You read it. You send it.

It is not magic. You still run the business. It just stops living in your head.

What we set up

The pieces

  • A jobs board: to estimate, scheduled, in progress, done.
  • Estimates in one place, with what's been paid.
  • To-dos and follow-ups with dates, so nothing goes quiet.
  • Claude, connected to the board, with instructions written for your business.
  • An hour at your desk showing you how it works. On your own phone.
